# Marvel Strike Force Planning

Trackers for events, resources and character builds.

## Files

- **Event_Tracker.xlsx** is the main tracker. Its tabs:
  - **Build Priority**: which characters to build next and why
  - **Tower Roster**: Tower Challenger III characters for the Kyln tower
  - **Iron Vengeance**: milestones, rewards and claim status
  - **Scoring**: Iron Vengeance tasks and how many times each is left
  - **Resources**: gold, purple ions and training materials, with what's coming in and what's planned
  - **Dailies**: daily checklist with gold totals
- **Iso_Fest_Tracker.xlsx** tracks Iso Fest. Its tabs:
  - **Iso Fest**: milestones and purple ion rewards
  - **Scoring**: point sources, with a planner that shows which milestone your planned spending reaches
- **Astral_Radiance_Tracker.xlsx** tracks the Astral Radiance event.
- **Iron_Vengeance_Tracker_Template.xlsx** is a blank Iron Vengeance tracker to share with others.

Yellow cells are the ones to fill in.
